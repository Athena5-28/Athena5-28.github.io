03 / About

About BioMem

↗Helsinki, Finland
60.17° N / 24.94° E

A molecular approach to the future of memory.

BioMem is an early-stage deep-tech project working at the intersection of protein engineering, nanotechnology, and computer architecture. We are currently developing and validating our first experimental memory-cell architectures in Helsinki.

We are exploring how engineered proteins and molecular-scale components can form functional elements of future memory systems. Our work spans protein design, molecular interfaces, semiconductor-compatible architectures, and experimental memory-cell prototypes.
